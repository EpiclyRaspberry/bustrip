How the estimate works

Road distance ÷ your average speed + the breaks you add. The route uses a driving profile, not bus-specific road restrictions or a scheduled bus service. Average speed includes driving slowdowns; add expected pickups or delays as a manual break.

Suggestions aim for a break about every 2–3 driving hours, with fewer stops on short trips. They use live map results and a small, source-linked starter guide for the southern Luzon corridor. Guide places appear only when near your actual road route. Food and toilet badges distinguish mapped facilities from listings or traveler reports. A restaurant is never assumed to have toilets. This is a planning aid, not an operator itinerary.
